CooldownCollaborator look board

Windows at raid scale, and the new pieces

Pick one look per idea, or None of these, and add a note. Every game screen is drawn from the addon’s real sizes and colours; the names are made up and letters stand in for spell art.

Windows at raid scale

From your note on the review list: review the frames design to be better scaled for raids. Each choice is drawn three times: a Retail Mythic+ key, a Retail 20-player raid and a Classic Era 40-player raid.

Main panel at raid scale

The list of every tracked cooldown someone has used; in a raid, READY rows fill its 14 rows and push the running timers out. Each new choice has a close button that stays shut and leaves flask and food to the Essentials bar.

Possible on every client. Retail and Forever: only players who run CooldownCollaborator show; Classic Era, TBC and MoP also read other players’ casts from the combat log. Today: ready first, then soonest, 14 rows, no close button, and closing it lasts until the next cast.

Essentials bar at raid scale

The bar for the Bloodlust/Heroism and Battle Rez providers, with the flask and food count: it has 10 lines, and a raid needs more.

Possible on every client; Classic Era and Forever have Battle Rez only (no lust there), and TBC has lust on Shamans only. Providers are counted by class, so strangers count too. Today: a heading and a line per provider, and in a raid the flask and food count adds you twice.

Lane view at raid scale

A lane each for Bloodlust/Heroism and Battle Rez, with each provider’s icon sliding from just used on the left to ready on the right.

Possible on every client; Classic Era and Forever have the Battle Rez lane only. Off until /cdc lanes. Today: 22 px icons under a 26 px colour square at 60%, green when ready, six icons a lane.

Party and raid layouts

Window places and sizes saved once for a party and once for a raid, switched when the group changes, with one button that puts every window back.

Possible on every client. Today: one saved place per window, /cdc reset moves only the main panel, and on a fresh install the Essentials bar sits 50 px over the main panel (red). Drawn on a 16:9 screen; grey boxes are the game’s own frames.

New pieces

Ideas from the feature panel that put something new on screen, two to four ways each. Each drawing’s small line says the client and group size.

Icon-only look

A panel style of spell icons with a cooldown sweep and a class-colour mark, packed tight, grey while cooling and bright when ready.

Possible on every client. Today: text rows, the Essentials bar and the Lane view.

Dead or offline owners

A ready cooldown whose owner is dead or offline shows greyed with a mark, and the Essentials bar names the next live provider.

Possible on every client (out of range only out of combat). Today: a dead or offline owner’s spell still shows READY, and offline players count as providers.

Dead list with a one-click rez call

When someone dies their name shows in a small strip; one click picks the best living rezzer and calls it, in instance chat in Group Finder groups, and anyone may call in a 5-player group.

Partly on Retail and Forever: who is ready comes only from players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: target the dead player, then the Rez button or /cdc rez; leader or assist only, party or raid chat.

Ready defensives as one number

A small tile on the Essentials bar counting the ready externals and raid defensives you pick, out of how many the group has, with who and what on hover.

Partly on Retail and Forever: counts only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: the Essentials bar has lust, Battle Rez and flask or food only.

Ready check and pull summary

On a ready check or when a pull countdown starts (the game’s or a boss mod’s), one short summary: lust ready or not, Battle Rez charges, externals ready, and anything still cooling.

Partly on Retail and Forever: only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: /cdc roster lists providers when typed; nothing reacts to a ready check or a pull.

Lust or Battle Rez missing or down to one

When the group forms or changes, one line says who brings lust and Battle Rez, and warns when there is none, only one, or the last one is dead or offline.

Possible on every client (no lust on Classic Era or Forever); it counts classes, so it works with strangers. Today: a missing lust just leaves its heading off the bar; No providers in group shows only when neither is there.

Flash when a chosen cooldown is ready

Pick spells in Settings and get a sound, a flash or both when one comes back, yours or a groupmate’s, with an off switch, a sound picker and an optional warning a few seconds before.

Partly on Retail and Forever: groupmates’ spells only if they run CooldownCollaborator. Today: a green READY, no sound or flash.

An external’s effect before its cooldown

When a tracked external or big defensive is cast, its row shows the effect’s own timer first (and on Classic who it went on), then the cooldown, so nobody stacks a second one.

Partly: on Retail and Forever only from players who run CooldownCollaborator, and the target is hidden there. Today: only the cooldown timer.

Guessed cooldowns

A player who doesn’t run the addon and hasn’t been seen casting gets a mark meaning ready as far as we know; confirmed lines (players who run it, your own spells) look normal.

Retail and Forever only; Classic Era, TBC and MoP see casts in the combat log. Today: a provider never seen casting always shows READY.

Who runs the addon, and why the panel is empty

Each copy says hello with its version when it joins a group, and /cdc guild asks the whole guild; the panel then shows who runs it, and an empty panel says why.

Possible on every client. Today: messages carry no version, and an empty panel says only No cooldowns tracked.

Hover text

Hovering a row, lane icon or Essentials line shows what the spell does, one plain line on when it matters, who owns it and when it’s back.

Possible on every client. Today: hovering a row only lights its class stripe white.

“You’re the rez”

When you are the group’s lust or Battle Rez provider, the Essentials bar says so, so you know the group is counting on you.

Possible on every client. Today: providers are listed by name, yours included, with nothing to tell yours apart.

Mana cooldowns together

A small strip for the mana tools (Mana Tide Totem, Innervate, Shadowfiend, Hymn of Hope) with who has one ready, and an ask button like Request Rez.

Full on Classic Era, TBC and MoP; Retail and Forever track few of these (Retail: Innervate; Forever: Innervate and Mana Tide Totem). Today: in the main list only.

What will be back for the next big hit

Type a time or use a pull timer, and the Lane view marks which cooldowns will be ready by then, and warns when none will.

Partly on Retail and Forever: only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: no look-ahead.

Next lust on one line

One short line or icon with the soonest lust and who holds it, usable instead of the whole Essentials bar.

Retail, TBC and MoP; no lust on Classic Era or Forever. Today: every lust provider on its own line.

Lust running and Sated timers

A timer for the lust buff on you and for Sated or Exhaustion, so you know when the burst ends and when a second lust is allowed.

Partly on Retail (your own buff and Sated only); not on Classic Era or Forever. Today: only the lust cooldown.

Burst window planner

Your own big cooldowns beside the group’s lust and Power Infusion, with a mark when they line up; you tick which of your spells count.

Partly on Retail and Forever: group timers only from players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: nothing combines your cooldowns with the group’s.

Externals that can reach me

A small strip of only the externals cast on another player (Guardian Spirit, Pain Suppression, Blessing of Sacrifice, Life Cocoon, Ironbark and the like) with owner and ready time.

Partly on Retail and Forever: only players who run CooldownCollaborator; Retail’s list would gain Blessing of Sacrifice. Today: one list of everything.

Getting Started tab and the CooldownTimeline3 link

Settings opens on a Getting Started tab with each window’s on/off, Lock and Reset, plus one line on what CooldownTimeline3 adds and where to get it, or a button into its Group cooldowns settings.

Possible on every client. Today: Settings opens on Display Settings, the Essentials bar and Lane view switch only by /cdc essentials and /cdc lanes, and the CooldownTimeline3 box shows only when it is loaded.

Raid cooldown plan

The leader writes who uses what and when (a time, or a boss timer from BigWigs or DBM) and shares it with the group; each player gets a prompt on their turn, and the panel flags a planned spell that won’t be back.

Partly on Retail and Forever: ready status only from players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: no plan, only the live list.

Rotation order for a cooldown

The leader sets an order for a spell; the panel marks the next ready player in line, moves on when it’s used, and the order can be shared with the group.

Partly on Retail and Forever: only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: no order; Request Rez takes the first ready provider.

Show only my raid team

Save named teams (a set of players) and pick one to show only them on the panel, Essentials bar and lanes; others are hidden, not forgotten.

Possible on every client. Today: everyone known in the group shows.

Guild-wide view

An optional guild mode, off by default, that also shares cooldowns over the guild channel, with a small list of who in the guild runs it and what they have ready.

Partly: only guild members who run CooldownCollaborator, on every client. Today: messages go only to the group (party, raid or instance).

Who has a Soulstone on them

A small list of who has a Soulstone on them, who placed it and how long is left, warning when a tank or healer has none or a stone is about to run out.

Partly: everyone on Classic Era and TBC; on Forever only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: only the warlock’s Soulstone cooldown.

World buffs and consumables strip

Strips like Flask and Food for world buffs (Dragonslayer, Zandalar, Songflower, Warchief’s Blessing, Dire Maul) and other consumables, with who lacks them; you choose which show.

Partly: everyone on Classic Era and TBC; on Forever only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: Flask and Food counts from players who run it, and in a raid you are counted twice.

Every way to bring someone back, in one row

One Resurrection row adding up ready Rebirths, placed Soulstones and Reincarnations, with the next to come back and a call button.

Partly: everyone on Classic Era and TBC; on Forever only players who run CooldownCollaborator. Today: Rebirth counts as Battle Rez; Soulstone is tracked but never listed as a rez, and Reincarnation isn’t tracked.

Battle Rez charges as one number

The Essentials bar shows the raid’s Battle Rez charges and the time to the next one, shared by whoever can read it, with the ready rezzers in order so the leader can pick.

Partly on Retail: the pool can be read only for your own spell, so a rezzer who runs CooldownCollaborator has to share it. Today: your own charges are read; nothing is shared or shown.
